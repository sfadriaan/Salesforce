## UI Element Listing

### Violations (Incorrect Usage)

Click the Login button to begin the session. 
After that, they must **click the Submit button** on the form. 
To finalize the process, be sure to **click the Finish button**. 
For a quick solution, we advise you to **click the Save button** immediately. 
If you need to revert, **click the Cancel button**.

### Correct Usage (Passing Style)

First, click **Login** to begin the session. 
After that, click **Submit** on the form. 
To finalize the process, click **Finish**. 
For a quick solution, click **Save** immediately. 
If you need to revert, click **Cancel**.

### Edge Cases (Should Not Violate)

1.  Clicking the big red button. (No "the" and no surrounding context)
2.  Click **the** big red **button**. (Incorrectly formatted, but still violates the pattern)
3.  Do not click **the button** without reading the warning. (Too generic)
