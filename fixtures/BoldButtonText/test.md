## 📝 Button Formatting Test Excerpt

### Incorrect (Rule Violations)

First, to begin the process, you must click Next to proceed to the configuration screen. 
After setting the parameters, **Click Run Report**. 
The system will not process your request until you **Click OK** on the confirmation dialog. 
Do not simply **Click Cancel** if you decide to abort; you must click the "Back" link instead. 
To duplicate the record, the user will click Clone directly on the detail page. 
If any changes are made, remember to click Save before navigating away. 
The old system used to simply say **Click Delete** to remove an entry.

### Correct (Passing Style)

After making the necessary changes, be sure to click **Save** to apply the updates immediately. 
Once the data is secured, you can then choose to click **Delete** if the record is no longer needed. 
To move to the next step, click **Next**. 
If you need a copy, use the action menu to click **Clone**. 
To dismiss the window without error, click **OK**. 
You may click **Cancel** at any time to discard changes and close the panel. 
When generating the output, you must click **Run Report** to start the job.
